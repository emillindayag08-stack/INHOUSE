Two ways a case closes early

If the notarized complaint isn't filed within its 3-day window, the Committee dismisses for non-compliance. If the Committee finds no prima facie case, it's dismissed there too — neither path ever reaches a formal charge.

Who picks the pathway

Complainant and Respondent each choose independently. If they agree, it's final. If they don't — or either stalls — the Secretariat sets or overrides the final pathway at any point before it locks in.

Formal Investigation stays Secretariat-heavy

Every hearing (Pre-Trial and Trial) now requires its own minutes on file before the investigation can end. The final outcome is recorded once it comes back from the University President's office — external to the system, no lane of its own.
